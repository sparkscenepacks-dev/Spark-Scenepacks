# 🚀 Future Feature Ideas for Spark Scenepacks

Here are the suggestions we discussed to enhance the platform. We can dive into any of these tomorrow!

## 1. Community Voting System
- **Concept**: A public feed of requests where users can upvote their favorites.
- **Goal**: Helps prioritize content based on community demand.

## 2. Creator of the Month Spotlight
- **Concept**: A featured section on the homepage for elite creators.
- **Goal**: Boosts community engagement and incentivizes high-quality uploads.

## 3. Editor's Toolkit (Tutorial Hub)
- **Concept**: A dedicated page for editing tutorials and resource links.
- **Goal**: Becomes a go-to destination for learning, not just downloading.

## 4. Interactive Content Roadmap
- **Concept**: A "Coming Soon" timeline showing what movies/shows are in progress.
- **Goal**: Transparency and hype-building for upcoming releases.

## 5. Collection Bundles
- **Concept**: Curated "Mega Packs" grouping related content.
- **Goal**: Simplifies the download process for specific genres or series.

## 6. User Bookmarking & Profiles
- **Concept**: Let users save their favorite packs to a personal list.
- **Goal**: Increases user retention and site personalization.

## 7. Live Community Stats
- **Concept**: Global counters for total downloads and active users.
- **Goal**: Social proof and showing the scale of the community.
